Hidden Gems in Munnar: Beyond the Tea Gardens

I just returned from a 10-day solo trip to the Western Ghats. While everyone flocks to the main tea estates, I found a secret trail leading to the 'Attukad Lower Falls' that isn't on most maps. The mist there at 6 AM is unlike anything I've ever seen—it feels like a different planet. Has anyone else explored the deeper valley trails lately? I'm looking for recommendations for my next visit in December.
